Before you buy

Your front faces east. It gets direct morning sun and falls into shade in the early afternoon, so drag the time slider past 1:30 PM to see how each color reads in shade.

Colors read lighter outside. A chip that looks right indoors often washes out on a sunny wall. Many people go one step darker on the fan deck.

LRV (light reflectance value) runs from 0 for black to 100 for white. Chips show the maker's LRV where published; a ~ marks an estimate from the screen color.

Primed Hardie board takes a 100% acrylic exterior paint. Paint a 2×2 ft sample board and look at it on the front porch and the south wall, morning and late afternoon.

The model is built from your photos and satellite imagery, so dimensions are estimates.
